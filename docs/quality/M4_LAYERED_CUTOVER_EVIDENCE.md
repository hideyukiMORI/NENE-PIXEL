# M4 Layered Cutover Evidence

This document records the device evidence for Issue #142 (P4-05a, the cutover to layered documents
with `Empty` cells and project v3, [ADR 0030](../adr/0030-ordered-layers-and-empty-pixels.md)).
Raw evidence lives in the development lab under `evidence/142-latency/` and
`evidence/142-device-check/` ([Evidence Locations](EVIDENCE_LOCATIONS.md)).

## Single-layer drawing latency — collected 2026-09-29, `PERFORMANCE_FAIL`

M5 drawing-latency budget of Issue #135 ([M2 frame follow-up](M2_FRAME_FOLLOW_UP.md)).

### Where the difference is

description of the collected population, not a second verdict.

| `canvas256_repeated_diagonal` | commit | 50 | 1.377 → 2.683 | 2.527 → 4.212 | 10.541 → 11.942 |

Preview frames did not change. Commit frames of the 256 × 256 document take about 1.3 to 1.4 ms
longer in the candidate. 46 of the 54 slowest candidate frames of that family (the top 6 percent)
are commit frames, so the all-frame p95 lies inside the commit-frame population. This matches the
stated hypothesis that the cutover adds one whole-document composite and bitmap rebuild per commit.
Whether the added time is the work itself or the missing Baseline Profile coverage of the new
composite path has not been measured.

### What follows

Issue #142 stays open and PR #146 is not merged on this evidence. The M5 budget and the Lane 3
tolerances are unchanged. A further collection runs under the third bounded rule of
[the P4 protocol](P4_INDEXED_CUTOVER_PROTOCOL.md): a revised plan in the Issue first, with a named
change to the commit path or to the profile, its expected effect and a new experiment id.

against the candidate production tree.

The owner confirmed on the device: undo and redo, the actual-size window, the eyedropper, save and

pixel as `(0, 0, 0, 0)` and every other pixel unchanged.
